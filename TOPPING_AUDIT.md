# Topping audit — November 3, 2026

Scope: all 23 catalog items and 141 selectable option values. This document separates source/automated inspection from visible browser results.

## Findings and corrections

- French toast: cream was attached only to the final slice and berries were beside the toast. Both now attach to each slice using slice-local positions and stable IDs for both two- and three-slice portions. Price remains once per dish.
- Maple Bacon Burger: catalog promised a maple finish but the recipe had none. Added a thin, fixed maple glaze on the patty, independent of removable house/smoky sauce. Cheese/bacon may naturally obscure it.
- Savory bread and French-toast support heights were slightly stale relative to current exported geometry. Updated measured heights.
- Explicit pour metadata now distinguishes syrup/fudge/drizzle/melted cheese from chocolate chips, nuts, berries, cinnamon, and breadcrumbs. Liquid animation is tested separately from static recipe placement.

## Evidence and limits

- `test/toppings.test.js` checks 298 individual topping add/remove cases across every single-choice combination, including exact topping-price deltas, per-slice garnish support, selected bread/cheese/protein/sauce/dip/scoop/ice geometry, and support-dependent drizzle.
- `test/food-model.test.js` additionally enumerates all 2,216 complete recipe configurations, checks local assets and transforms, and requires a distinct recipe for each configuration. Recipe differences alone do not prove visible correctness.
- Recipe source was reviewed for all 23 items: pancake garnish stays atop the whole stack, french-toast garnish belongs to each separate slice, sundae fudge coats each scoop, dips remain in a cup, and toppings follow drink/side serving sizes.
- Directly inspected fresh Blender renders: French-toast loaded three-slice serving, Maple Bacon loaded/minimal burger, and 1000px loaded Mac & Cheese and Hot Fudge Sundae. All three toast slices visibly have supported cream and berries; maple burger has correctly stacked selectable toppings with natural internal occlusion. Mac bacon pieces and toasted crumbs are small but visibly resting on pasta, not buried; sundae nuts are visible around the upper scoop outside the cream. No placement change was warranted for those two garnishes.
- Browser interaction, every individual toggle in the actual viewer, and live publication are **not verified by this document yet**. Root is handling integrated browser checks; saved browser permission was initially blocked. Offline renders do not substitute for browser interaction or animation verification.
- Root inspected all 69 default/minimal/loaded renders for all 23 dishes. Topping and serving differences were visible; the low-resolution uncertainty around Mac & Cheese and sundae garnish was resolved by the larger images above. These are offline appearance checks, not browser or live-animation checks.
